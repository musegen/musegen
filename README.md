### 🎵 What is MuseGen

**Say it in a song.** MuseGen is an AI music generator that turns a text prompt,
an uploaded audio file or your own lyrics into a finished track — with vocals or
as pure instrumental — and can generate a music video to go with it.

It runs entirely in the browser. No DAW, no plugins, no install. Commercial use
is covered by our
**[Commercial License](https://www.musegen.ai/legal/commercial-license)**.

### 🌍 Why we build this

Most people who want a song don't want a studio. They have a moment — a
birthday, a product launch, a scene that needs scoring, a feeling at 2am — and
between that moment and a finished track sit years of instruments, theory,
mixing and mastering. Almost everyone gives up somewhere in that gap.

**MuseGen exists to close it. Describe a moment, get a real song back — vocals
and all.** No instruments, no music theory, no mixing desk, no sound engineer.
The work that used to need a producer happens behind a text box, and what comes
out is meant to be finished, not a sketch you still have to rescue.

We build it for everyone with something to say, not only for musicians: content
creators scoring reels, game and app developers who need loops that react,
podcasters after an intro, businesses building a sound of their own, and people
writing one song for one person. That audience isn't concentrated in a single
market, which is why the platform ships in **seven languages** — English,
French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian and Simplified Chinese.

And the engine keeps moving. **V6 is live**, with steadier vocals and faster
generation. The promise stays the same while the output gets better — that's the
whole job.

### 🧰 The prompt-to-song toolchain

Three open-source tools we maintain. All of them are **tool-neutral** — they work
with Suno, Udio, Stable Audio and MuseGen alike.

```
awesome-music-prompts  →  music-prompt-skill  →  lyrics-generator-mcp  →  musegen.ai
   96 prompts to              vague request         brief → title +          generate
   copy and adapt            → usable prompt       tagged full lyrics        the track
```

| Repo | What it does |
|---|---|
| **[awesome-music-prompts](https://github.com/musegen/awesome-music-prompts)** | A copy-pasteable prompt library — 96 annotated prompts across genre, mood, vocal, tempo and use case. Written to fit real style-field length limits. |
| **[music-prompt-skill](https://github.com/musegen/music-prompt-skill)** | A Claude Skill that turns a vague music request into a specific, production-ready prompt. |
| **[musegen-lyrics-generator-mcp](https://github.com/musegen/musegen-lyrics-generator-mcp)** | An MCP server for songwriting: multilingual lyrics with English section tags (`[Verse]`, `[Chorus]`) ready to paste into any music generator. Runs locally with your own API key. |

Free prompt templates by genre also live on the site:
**[60 ready-to-use templates](https://www.musegen.ai/ai-music-prompts)** across 12 genres.

### 🔌 Is there an API?

**No public or developer API.** MuseGen is a web product — the browser UI is the
whole surface, and there are no API keys to request.

The closest thing to programmatic access is
**[musegen-lyrics-generator-mcp](https://github.com/musegen/musegen-lyrics-generator-mcp)**,
which runs locally and generates lyrics via your own Anthropic key. Track
generation itself stays in the app.
